Digital Financial Safety & Fraud Awareness

Protect your village enterprise and hard-earned savings from cyber criminals and online fraudsters.

The #1 Golden Rule of UPI Payments

You NEVER need to enter your UPI PIN to RECEIVE or ACCEPT money from a customer!

If anyone asks you to scan a QR code or enter your 4-digit/6-digit secret PIN to "receive payment", "claim prize", or "receive government subsidy", it is 100% A FRAUD. Entering your PIN always sends money OUT of your bank account.

1. "Request Money" UPI Scam

A fraudster calls saying they want to buy goods from your shop and will pay advance via UPI. Instead of sending money, they send a "Collect Request" notification.

Protection: Decline any "Collect Request" from strangers. Real buyers scan your QR code or send to your mobile number; you never click 'Approve' to get money.
2. Fake Bank Officer / OTP Fraud

A caller claims your ATM card is blocked or your KYC has expired. They speak authoritatively and ask for your 6-digit OTP received via SMS.

Protection: No bank employee, police officer, or government official will EVER ask for your OTP. Never share your OTP with anyone, not even friends.
3. Tampered QR Code at Counter

Fraudsters visit small shops as fake customers and discreetly stick their own printed QR code sticker over the shopkeeper's genuine payment QR standee.

Protection: Check your shop QR code every morning. Keep soundbox / audio confirmation active so you immediately hear payments coming into YOUR account.
4. Instant Fake Loan Apps

Apps advertised on social media promise loans in 5 minutes without documents. Once installed, they steal your phone contacts, gallery photos, and blackmail you with exorbitant interest rates.

Protection: Never download unknown APKs from WhatsApp or social media. Apply for loans only through RBI-registered banks or official portals like MUDRA.
5. Phishing SMS & Lottery Links

You receive an SMS saying: "Congratulations, you won Rs. 25,00,000 in KBC / PM Subsidy. Click this link to claim."

Protection: Never tap on links from unknown numbers. Government schemes never disburse lottery cash over WhatsApp or unofficial links.
6. "Double Your Money" Chit Funds

Unregistered agents promise to double your deposit in 6 months or 1 year. After collecting money from several villagers, the operator vanishes.

Protection: Save only with scheduled commercial banks, post office schemes, or registered SHGs under government federations.

Quick Safety Checklist: Do's & Don'ts

DO (Safe Habits) DON'T (Never Do This)
Set a strong UPI and ATM PIN and keep it secret. Never write your PIN on your ATM card or mobile cover.
Verify the name displayed on screen before sending payments. Never enter your UPI PIN to receive money or lottery winnings.
Use soundbox or verify bank SMS after customer payments. Never rely on customer's mobile screenshot without checking your account.
Report suspicious calls immediately to National Helpline 1930. Never install screen-sharing apps (AnyDesk, TeamViewer) on caller request.

Got Defrauded or Received a Scam Call?

Act within the "Golden Hour" (first 2 hours) to maximize the chance of freezing fraudulent money transfers.

Call Cyber Helpline 1930 (Toll Free) File Complaint at cybercrime.gov.in →
